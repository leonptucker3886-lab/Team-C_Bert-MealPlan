# Competitors

| App | Price | Strengths | Weaknesses |
|---|---|---|---|
| Mealime | Free / Pro subscription | Polished recipes, auto grocery list | Tied to its own recipe catalog, extras need a subscription |
| Paprika | Paid per platform | Recipe import, planner, pantry | Pay again on each device, dated UI |
| Plan to Eat | Monthly/yearly subscription | Import any recipe, drag-and-drop planner | Subscription only |

## MealPlan's angle
- One-time $4.99, no subscription, no account
- Your own meals, not a fixed catalog
- Grocery list merges duplicate ingredients and sums quantities
- Works offline in any browser, with backups
